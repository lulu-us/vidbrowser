## 🚀 How to Use VidBrowser

Getting started is straightforward.

1. **Visit the website:** Open [VidBrowser](https://vidbrowser.app) in your browser.
2. **Find your video:** Navigate to the webpage containing the video you want to save.
3. **Check compatibility:** Make sure the website and video are supported by VidBrowser.
4. **Download your video:** Follow the instructions provided by the website to save the available video.
5. **Watch offline:** Access your downloaded video locally whenever you need it.

*The exact workflow and availability of downloads depend on the source website, video format, access restrictions, and current VidBrowser functionality.*
